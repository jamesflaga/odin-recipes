Filipino Lumpia Shanghai

Description

Filipino Lumpia Shanghai, or Lumpiang Shanghai, is a popular Filipino appetizer consisting of ground pork or poultry mixed with minced vegetables, wrapped in a this pasty skin, and deep-fried until golden and crispy.

Ingredients

Filling

  • Ground Pork: 1 pound (choose regular fat content, not lean, to keep the folling juicy).
  • Onion: 1/2 cup, very finely minced.
  • Carrot: 1/2 cup, finely minced or grated.
  • Garlic: 2 to 3 cloves, mined or grated.
  • Green Onions / scallions: 1/4 to 1/2 cup, finely chopped.
  • Egg: 1 large (mixed into meat to bind it).
  • Soysauce: 1 teaspoon.
  • Salt & black pepper: 1 teaspoon each (adjust to taste).

Wrapping and Frying

  • Lumpia wrappers: 1 package (think spring roll wrappers, thawed if frozen; do not use thick egg roll wrappers).
  • Water or egg white: A small amount in a bowl to seal the edges.
  • Cooking oil: 2 cups or enough for shallow/deep frying (canola or vegetable oil).

Instructions

  1. Mix the Filling

    1. Place the gound pork, minced, minced onion, carros, galic, and green onions into a large mixing bowl.
    2. Add the egg, soy sauce, salt and black pepper.
    3. Mix everything thoughoughlu with your hands until the ingredients are well combined.

  2. Wrap the Lumpia

    1. Carefully peel apart the lumpia wrappers and lay them on a clean, dry surface. Cover the stack with a slightly damp towel so they do not dry out.
    2. Separate the one wrapper and place it in a diamond shape (with one corner pointing toward you)
    3. Place about 1 to 2 tablespoon of meat filling in a small log shape accross the lower third of the wrapper, leaving a space at both sides and the bottom.
    4. Leave the bottom corner tighly over the filling, fold the left and right outer corners inward towards the center, and roll it up firmly into a tight cylinder.
    5. Dip your finger in water or egg white and lightly dab the top corner to seal the edge closed. Repeat until all filling is used. keeping finished rolls covered.

  3. Fry and Serve

    1. Heat about 1 inch of cooking oil in a large skillet over medium high heat (around 350°F to 375°).
    2. Place the lumpia into hot oil in small batches, seam side-down, making sure not to crowd the pan.
    3. Fry, turning occassionally, until all side s are golden brown and the pork inside is fully cooked (about 3 to 5 minutes).
    4. Remove the rolls with a slotted spoon or tongs and drain then on a plate lined with paper towels.
    5. Serve hot with sweet chili sauce, banana ketchup, or spiced vinegar.
