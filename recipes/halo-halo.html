Halo Halo

Description

Halo-Halo is the ultimate Filipino shaved ice dessert, famous for its vibrant layers, contrasting textures, and colorfula ppearance. The name literally translates to "mix-mix" in Tagalog, which is exactly how it meant to be eaten.

Ingredients

You can find most of these ingredients jarred and pre-sweetened at any local Asian or Filipino grocery.

The Base & Liquids

  • 2 cups shaved or finely crushed ice (essential for the right texture)
  • 1/2 cup evaporated milk
  • 1 to 2 tablespoons simple syrup or condensed milk (optiona, for extra sweetness)

The Layered Fillings (Mix and Match):

  • 2 tablespoons sweetened red mung beans
  • 2 tablespoons sweetened white beans or chickpeas
  • 2 tablespoons kaong (sugar palm fruit in syrup)
  • 2 tablespoons nata de coco (coconut gel cubes)
  • 2 tablespoons macapuno (sweetened cocnut sport strips)
  • 2 tablespoons sweetened jackfruit (langka), sliced into strips
  • 2 tablespoons saba bananas, sweetened and sliced
  • 2 tablespoons red and green agar-agar jelly, cut into small cubes
  • 2 tablespoons tapioca pearls (sago), cooked

Premiun Toppings

  • 1 large scoop ube (purple yam) ice cream
  • 1 large leche flan (Filipino caramel custard)
  • 1 tablespoon ube halaya (purple yan jam)
  • 1 teaspoon pinipig (toasted pounded rice flakes) or corn flakes for crunch

Instructions

  1. Layer the Bottom Sweets

    In a tall glass or a large clear glass bowl (like the one shown in your image), start adding your bottom layers. Spoon 1 to 2 tablespoons of each of your chosen heavy ingredients into the bottom. Start with the beans, followed by the gelatin cubes, nata de coco, kaong, tapioca pearls, jackfruit, and bananas.

  2. Pack the Shaved Ice and Pour the Milk

    Pack the glass or bowl almost to the top with freshly shaved or finely crushed ice. Gently pack it down so it holds its shape but leaves enough room for the milk to seep through.

  3. Add the Showstopping Toppings

    Slowly pour the evaporated milk all over the shaved ice. The milk will seep down through the ice crystals, sweetening and flavoring the ice and bottom layers. If you prefer it extra sweet, drizzle a tablespoon of condensed milk or simple syrup over the top of the ice now.

  4. Add the Showstopping Toppings

    Carefully place your premium toppings right on top of the ice mound:

    1. Add a generous scoop of ube ice cream right in the middle.
    2. Nestle a slice of rich leche flan right next to the ice scream.
    3. Add a dollop of ube halaya if using
    4. Finish by sprinkling the pinipig or cornflakes all over the top for that perfect crunchy texture.

  5. Mix-Mix and Enjoy!

    Serve immediately with a long spoon. To eat it like a local, use your spoon to plunge straight to the bottom and vigorously stir and mix everything together until the ice melts slightly into a thick, creamy, purple, and colorful sweet slurry.
