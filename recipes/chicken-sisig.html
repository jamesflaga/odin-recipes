Chicken Sisig

Description

One of the most delicately catered and popular dish in the Philippines—Chicken Sisig. This is perfect for daily consumption and is always present in Filipino parties. One plate of Chicken Sisig can feed upto eight people. This perfectly panered with rice and a refresher soda.

Ingredients

  • 4 cups cooked or grilled chicken (chopped fine, including skin for texture)
  • 1/4 pound chicken livers
  • 1/2 cup canlia or cooking oil
  • 3 cloves garlic minced
  • 1 large onion, peeled and chopped
  • 2 to 3 Thai chili peppers (siling labuyo) or jalapeõs, chopped
  • 1/4 cup calamansi juice (or substitute line/lemon juice)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce or Knorr liquid seasoning
  • 1/4 cup mayonnaise
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp butter and 1 egg (optional, for serving a hot sizzling plate)

Instructions

  1. Crisp the Liver: Heat a small amount of oil in a pan over medium heat. Add the chicken livers and cook until browned and crispy. Remove them, let them cool, and chop or mash them into small bits.
  2. Brown the Chicken: In a wide pan, heat 1 tablespoon of oil over medium-high heat. Add half the minced garlic and the chopped chicken. Cook and turn until the chicken turns light brown and slightly crispy at the edges.
  3. Season the Base: Stir in the soy sauce or liquid seasoning, letting it absorb into the chicken for about 30 seconds, then remove from heat.
  4. Mix the Ingredients: In a large bowl, combine the cooked chicken, chopped liver, chopped onions, and chili peppers.
  5. Add Citrus adn Mayo: Pour in the calamansi or citrus juice, season with salt and pepper, then gently fold in the mayonnaise until well incorporated.
  6. Serve Sizzling (Optional): Heat a cast-iron sizzling plate with a tablespoon of butter. Transfer the chicken sisig mixture onto the hot plate, crack a raw egg right on top, and serve immediately while it sizzles.
